Examination information

In Presenter Mode, typing the key does not reveal it to students. Turn on “Show centre key” only when ready.

You can prepare remarks in advance. They will only appear on the projector when you enable the toggle.

Appearance settings

Font sizes

Adjust each display independently. Presenter Mode has larger default text for TV projection.

Colours

Franklin Gothic Medium is the default. Segoe UI, Tahoma and Bahnschrift are available as alternatives. The examination centre key always uses Consolas. Fonts must be installed on the displaying computer.

Set up Presenter Mode

Keep the controls on your laptop while students see only the examination display.

  1. Connect your projector or TV to your laptop.
  2. Press Win + P and select Extend (not Duplicate).
  3. Click Open presentation window below.
  4. Drag the new window by its title bar onto the projector or TV.
  5. Double-click the display to enter full screen. Double-click again to exit. If necessary, use F11.

The window opens in a movable size first, so you can place it on the correct screen. Browser security may prevent automatic full screen; double-clicking is the reliable shortcut.
